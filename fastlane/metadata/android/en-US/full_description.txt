Nexora is a free, open-source music player built around audio quality and a liquid-glass design.

AUDIO
- Musicolet-style equalizer tuned to avoid harshness and listening fatigue
- 8D and 16D audio modes with a fold-away control strip
- Real-time song trend analysis and an Ask AI assistant (bring your own key)

PLAYBACK
- Local files and streaming, gapless queue, saved mixes
- Synced lyrics on the player
- Notch media pill that wraps around your camera cutout (optional, off by default)

DESIGN
- Liquid-glass UI with a bottom pill player and a dynamic notch-area player
- Dark-first theming

No ads, no tracking. Nexora is a GPL-3.0 fork of LastWave, actively maintained.
